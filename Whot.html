Whot!

The Nigerian card game. Match the shape or the number, empty your hand first.

How to play
  • Play a card that matches the shape or the number of the top card.
  • No card to play? Tap the deck to pick one. Your turn ends.
  • 1 Hold on: play again. 8 Suspension: play again.
  • 2 Pick two. 5 Pick three. Answer with the same number to pass it on, or pick.
  • 14 General market: opponent picks one, you play again.
  • 20 Whot plays on anything. You choose the next shape.
  • First to empty their hand wins.
